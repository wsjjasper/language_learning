# 场景库

`/weekly-review` 每周从这里挑场景写进下周计划，按当前月份的阶段（M1/M2/M3）由易到难。用过的场景会在 `progress/weekly/` 里留下记录，尽量不重复。可以随时往这里加自己真实遇到的场景，那些最有价值。

## 工作：听力（LISTENING）

| 阶段 | 场景 |
|---|---|
| M1 | Daily standup: 3 engineers give updates, one is blocked on another team |
| M1 | Two coworkers chatting before a meeting starts (weekend, kids, a game last night) |
| M1 | Manager announces a reorg / priority change in a team sync |
| M2 | Sprint planning: PM and 2 engineers argue about scope, people talk over each other |
| M2 | Incident retro: blame-free but tense; someone is defensive |
| M2 | Lunch table banter about a new AI tool, tech layoffs, and stock prices |
| M2 | Zoom call with bad connection: people cut out, "you're on mute", talking over each other |
| M3 | Design review with an Indian-English-speaking architect and a Southern US PM |
| M3 | Skip-level with a director who speaks in vague corporate-speak (what do they *really* mean?) |
| M3 | Vendor/partner call where the other side is evasive |

## 工作：口语（SPEAKING）

| 阶段 | 场景 |
|---|---|
| M1 | Give your standup update; coworker asks follow-up questions |
| M1 | 1:1 with manager: explain what you're working on and ask for help on a blocker |
| M1 | Explain a bug you fixed to a non-technical PM |
| M1 | Introduce yourself to a new teammate and explain your team's system |
| M2 | Design review: propose a change, a skeptical senior engineer pushes back |
| M2 | Push back on a PM's deadline without sounding negative |
| M2 | Jump into a fast meeting discussion to make your point (you'll be interrupted) |
| M2 | Ask a clarifying question when you didn't follow a discussion, without looking lost |
| M3 | Walk through trade-offs of two approaches, impromptu, no prep |
| M3 | Performance review: talk about your impact and ask for promotion feedback |
| M3 | Disagree with a staff engineer in a meeting, then reach a compromise |
| M3 | Run a meeting: keep it on track, cut someone off politely, summarize action items |

## 生活（LIFE）

### Small talk
| 阶段 | 场景 |
|---|---|
| M1 | Monday morning "How was your weekend?" at the coffee machine |
| M1 | Chat with a neighbor about the weather, the HOA, home repairs |
| M1 | Kid's school pickup: chat with another parent |
| M2 | Coworker talks about the NFL / NBA game last night (you didn't watch it) |
| M2 | Elevator chat with someone from another team: keep it going for 60 seconds |
| M3 | Holiday plans chat (Thanksgiving, Super Bowl party, July 4th) |

### 打电话办事
| 阶段 | 场景 |
|---|---|
| M1 | Call a restaurant to book a table / ask about a wait |
| M1 | Schedule a doctor's appointment (new patient, insurance questions) |
| M2 | Call insurance to dispute a bill; rep is impatient and uses jargon (deductible, copay, EOB) |
| M2 | Call internet provider to cancel or negotiate a lower price |
| M2 | Car service center explains what's wrong with your car and the cost |
| M3 | Call a pharmacy about a prescription that isn't ready; on hold and transferred |
| M3 | Report a lost card to the bank, with identity verification questions |

### 社交娱乐
| 阶段 | 场景 |
|---|---|

| M2 | Happy hour after work: people joke, tease each other, reference TV shows |
| M2 | Coworkers discuss a show everyone's watching (spoiler etiquette, memes) |
| M3 | Party where you know only the host: introduce yourself, join a group conversation |
| M3 | Someone tells a joke built on a pun / cultural reference; respond naturally |
