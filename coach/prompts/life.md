# 生活场景日（周五，30 分钟）

全程 Claude Project 语音模式。三类场景轮换：**small talk / 打电话办事 / 社交娱乐**。

## 开场

```
LIFE. Scenario: <从本周计划里挑，例如 "calling my insurance to dispute a bill">.
Your role: <对方，例如 "a slightly impatient customer service rep">.
Make it realistic: <phone: put me on hold, ask for my member ID, mishear me / small talk: drop a sports or pop-culture reference / social: tease me a little, make a joke I need to catch>.
Don't correct me until I say "feedback".
```

## 流程

1. **第 1 轮**（10 分钟）：完整走完场景。
2. **反馈**（5 分钟）：说 "feedback"。特别问一句：`What did a native speaker expect me to say that I didn't?`
3. **第 2 轮**（10 分钟）：同一场景换个变化重来，例如 "same call, but this time the rep says my claim was denied" 或 "same party, different person, they're really into football"。
4. **卡片**（5 分钟）：说 "card"，复制 SESSION CARD → `progress/inbox.md`。

## 小技巧

- small talk 的目标不是信息准确，而是 **接得住、能抛回去**：回答 + 一个细节 + 反问。
- 打电话时练这些救命句：
  - "Sorry, could you spell that for me?"
  - "Just to make sure I got that right, ..."
  - "Can you slow down a bit? The connection's not great."
- 听不懂梗时直接问："Wait, what's that from?"。美国人通常很乐意解释，这本身就是 small talk。
